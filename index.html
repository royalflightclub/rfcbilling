Department Billing

Royal Flight Club · App Created By: Micha Christine Dotimas

1. Upload the department ledger

Excel files are read on this computer. Ledger contents are not uploaded to GitHub or sent to other visitors.

Choose a file exported by your club system.

Default: current month only. Opening and closing balances are shown separately. Check the ledger convention against your source system.

2. Invoice details

Amounts are copied from the ledger. No VAT or other charges are added. Confirm required billing details before issuing. Invoice records and settings are stored in this browser only.

Print using A4 portrait. Turn off browser Headers and footers. A new issued bill gets a permanent number; printing it again keeps that number.

Upload a ledger to preview the invoice.

Issued bills

Saved on this device only. Export a backup before moving computers or clearing browser data. Bill numbers use a short sequence, for example RFC-2026-00001. Use one designated billing computer to avoid duplicate numbers across devices.

Install Royal Flight Club · Department Billing

Install on Windows using Microsoft Edge or Google Chrome.

On your computer

  1. Open the app link in Microsoft Edge or Google Chrome.
  2. Click Install app here, or the install icon in the browser address bar. Confirm Install.
  3. Launch Royal Flight Club · Department Billing from the Start menu. You can pin it to your taskbar.

In Edge you can also open the ⋯ menu → More tools → Apps → Install this site as an app.

Install on an Android phone
  1. Open the app in Chrome.
  2. Tap Install app and confirm Install.
  3. Launch it from your phone’s apps screen.

Your installed app opens in its own window. If installation is unavailable here, use the app link in Edge or Chrome.
